# Theme: Daylight

**Status:** v1 — fresh redesign direction (user asked for "something new" after
rejecting the prior two). Delivered as a real Figma file.
**Inspiration:** Built from scratch after auditing the old app's weaknesses
(card-soup Passport, buried CTAs, crude View-primitive icons, generic scaffold).
Confident, optimistic, high-clarity — not the timid lilac, not the loud green.
**Figma:** `SkillBridge — Daylight Redesign` — fileKey `CdQ2pSZ5CnZaoGp9MRHWri`
(https://www.figma.com/design/CdQ2pSZ5CnZaoGp9MRHWri). Three captured screens:
Welcome (1-2), Jobs (2-2), Passport (3-2).
**Mockups:** `sketch-mockup/daylight.html` (combined) + `daylight-welcome/jobs/passport.html` (single-screen, Figma-captured).

## Visual Language
- **Mood:** confident, optimistic, trustworthy. "Find work you can trust."
- **One hero action per screen:** Welcome → amber "Get started"; Jobs → amber
  "Apply with Passport" sticky bar (shield + "One tap · no resume needed");
  Passport → violet "Share Passport" hero.
- **Block hierarchy** instead of card soup: the Passport identity is a bold
  violet gradient hero block (digital ID card), not a plain stacked card.
- **Immersive gradient Welcome** establishes brand warmth immediately.
- **Spotlight bottom nav:** active tab = violet filled icon + underline.
- **Big friendly filled icons** (replacing the crude View-primitive set).
- Khmer-first, large tap targets, honest empty states carried over.

## Contrast with the other two themes
| Axis | Connected Calm | Market Pulse | Daylight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Anchor | Soft Purple `#6B5B95` | Fiverr Green `#1DBF73` | Indigo Violet `#5B3FA0` |
| Apply action | Gold (separate) | Green (brand) | Amber (warm, optimistic) |
| Feel | Calm / relaxed | Energetic / bold | Confident / optimistic |
| Identity block | Flat white card | Flat white card | Violet gradient hero |

## When to use
Default worker-app redesign candidate. Best when you want energy + trust without
the corporate coldness of navy or the loudness of pure marketplace green.
