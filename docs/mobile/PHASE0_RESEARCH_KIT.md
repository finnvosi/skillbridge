# SkillBridge Phase 0 — Field Validation Kit

**Status:** Draft v1 — for review by a native Khmer speaker before field use
**Companion doc:** `docs/mobile/WORKER_APP_BLUEPRINT.md` (§2 hypotheses, §16 test plan)
**Gate:** Proceed to Phase 1/2 build-out **only if** the validation targets in §10 are met.

> No worker interviews or usability tests have been completed for this product.
> This kit is the first field instrument. Everything here is designed to be
> run by a facilitator + one Khmer interpreter (or a bilingual facilitator),
> recorded with consent, and scored against the targets in §10.

---

## 1. Purpose

Phase 0 answers four questions before we invest further in the product:

1. **Do workers recognize the job-scam/uncertainty problem** strongly enough to change behavior for a verified alternative?
2. **Do workers understand the proposed proof** ("job checked / company checked / identity checked")?
3. **Can workers complete the core prototype without coaching** (find → assess → apply → report)?
4. **Does the language work in Khmer** (terms, trust labels, Passport metaphor)?

The gate in the blueprint (§14 Phase 0):

> *Proceed only if workers recognize the trust problem, understand the proposed
> proof, and can complete the core prototype without coaching.*

## 2. Hypotheses under test

From the blueprint (§2). Each interview should collect evidence for/against:

| # | Hypothesis |
|---|-----------|
| H1 | Workers experience enough uncertainty/fraud in factory recruitment to change behavior for a verified alternative |
| H2 | Workers will trust SkillBridge verification when the proof is explained clearly |
| H3 | Phone-number onboarding is more accessible than mandatory email onboarding |
| H4 | Salary, take-home pay, location, transport, shift, overtime, benefits, and fees are the first facts used to judge a factory job |
| H5 | Workers understand a passport metaphor and value a portable verified employment record |
| H6 | Employers will spend time verifying past employment records |
| H7 | Skill-based explanations improve application quality without discouraging capable workers |

## 3. Recruitment plan

### Quotas (15 workers minimum)

| Dimension | Target |
|---|---|
| Locations | Phnom Penh, Kandal, Kampong Speu, + one additional factory area |
| Age bands | 26–34 and 35–45 (both) |
| Gender | Strong majority women (garment workforce ≈ 80% women) |
| Employment status | Mix: employed, active job seeker, recent job changer (within 12 months) |
| Phone access | Mix: personal smartphone, shared phone, feature phone |

### Secondary interviews

- **5 factory HR / recruiters** (hiring practices, verification burden, willingness to verify records)
- **3 verification/support operators** (internal or partner; report handling, verification ops feasibility)

### Screening questions (worker)

Use to qualify participants *before* the full interview (see §5 for the full instrument).

1. Do you work or have you worked in a factory? (Yes → continue)
2. In the last 12 months, have you looked for a new factory job? (Yes → continue)
3. Age band: 26–34 or 35–45?
4. Where do you live/work? (Phnom Penh / Kandal / Kampong Speu / other factory area)
5. What phone do you use? (personal / shared / feature)

### Incentives

Recommend a small cash or phone-credit incentive (~$5–10 or equivalent), disclosed
at recruitment, paid after the session. If incentives are not possible, disclose
clearly that participation is voluntary.

### Ethics

- Read the consent script (§4) aloud in Khmer; get verbal consent recorded on tape.
- Participation is voluntary; the participant may stop at any time.
- No real job is offered, no payment is requested, and no personal documents are collected.
- Recordings are stored locally, de-identified in the report (first-name-only pseudonyms), and deleted after analysis unless the participant agrees otherwise.
- Reports and analytics never include uploaded documents, government identifiers, report narratives, or Passport content (blueprint §15).

English (for the record):

> Welcome to our study. We are building an app that helps factory workers find
> safer, verified jobs. This conversation takes about 45 minutes. We will ask
> about your work and job-search experience. Participation is voluntary — you

## 5. Worker interview guide

Run in Khmer. Ask one question at a time, wait, then probe. **Do not lead.**
"Tell me more" / "ហេតុអ្វី?" are the best probes. Mark quotes verbatim.

### A. Job search behavior

| # | Question (English) | សំណួរ (ខ្មែរ) | Probe |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Tell us about the last time you looked for factory work. | សូមប្រាប់យើងអំពីលើកចុងក្រោយដែលអ្នកស្វែងរកការងាររោងចក្រ។ | How long did it take? Who helped? |
| A2 | Where did you first hear about the job? | តើអ្នកបានឮអំពីការងារនេះដំបូងពីណា? | Facebook? friend? broker? factory gate? |
| A3 | What made you trust or distrust it? | តើអ្វីធ្វើឱ្យអ្នកជឿ ឬមិនជឿការងារនោះ? | Signals they look for |
| A4 | Did anyone ask you for money or documents before you were sure the job was real? | តើមាននរណាម្នាក់សុំលុយ ឬឯកសារពីអ្នក មុនពេលអ្នកប្រាកដថាការងារពិតប្រាកដដែរឬទេ? | Amount? outcome? |
| A5 | Which job facts did you check first? | តើព័ត៌មានណាខ្លះដែលអ្នកពិនិត្យមុនគេ? | Pay, location, shift, transport… (H4) |

### B. Trust model

| # | Question | សំណួរ | Probe |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | What would prove that a recruiter and a vacancy are genuine? | តើអ្វីអាចបញ្ជាក់ថាអ្នកជ្រើសរើស និងការងារពិតប្រាកដ? | Free-list the proofs (H2) |
| B2 | Show the verification label "Job checked". What do you think this means? | បង្ហាញស្លាក "ការងារបានផ្ទៀងផ្ទាត់"។ តើអ្នកគិតថាវាមានន័យយ៉ាងណា? | Test comprehension of the label |
| B3 | "SkillBridge checked the company and this vacancy." Does this mean the job is 100% safe? | "SkillBridge បានពិនិត្យក្រុមហ៊ុន និងតួនេះ។" តើនេះមានន័យថាការងារមានសុវត្ថិភាព ១០០% ដែរឬទេ? | Target: 90% answer "no" (§10) |
| B4 | If a job post says "no payment required to apply", what does that tell you? | បើការប្រកាសការងារនិយាយថា "មិនតម្រូវឱ្យបង់ប្រាក់ដើម្បីដាក់ពាក្យ" តើនេះប្រាប់អ្នកអ្វីខ្លះ? | Scam awareness |

### C. Passport metaphor

| # | Question | សំណួរ | Probe |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | How do you show a new employer what work you have done before? | តើអ្នកបង្ហាញអ្នកជ្រើសរើសថ្មីថាអ្នកធ្លាប់ធ្វើការអ្វីខ្លះដោយរបៀបណា? | CV? word of mouth? nothing? |
| C2 | Would an employer you trust confirming your past work help you? | តើការបញ្ជាក់ពីអតីតអ្នកជួលដែលអ្នកទុកចិត្ត អំពីការងារមុនរបស់អ្នក អាចជួយអ្នកបានទេ? | Value of verified record (H5) |
| C3 | We call this a "Career Passport". What do you think it contains? | យើងហៅនេះថា "លិខិតឆ្លងអាជីព"។ តើអ្នកគិតថាវាមានអ្វីខ្លះ? | Metaphor comprehension |
| C4 | Who would you allow to see that information? | តើអ្នកអនុញ្ញាតឱ្យនរណាមើលព័ត៌មាននេះខ្លះ? | Sharing boundaries (privacy) |

### D. Reporting & safety

| # | Question | សំណួរ | Probe |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | If someone asked you to pay for a job, would you report it? To whom? | បើមានគេសុំឱ្យអ្នកបង់លុយដើម្បីការងារ តើអ្នកនឹងរាយការណ៍ទេ? ទៅអ្នកណា? | Current reporting channels |
| D2 | Would you report inside an app if you knew the recruiter could not see your report? | តើអ្នកនឹងរាយការណ៍ក្នុងកម្មវិធី បើអ្នកដឹងថាអ្នកជ្រើសរើសមើលមិនឃើញរបាយការណ៍របស់អ្នកទេ? | Trust in anonymous reporting |

### E. Onboarding & phone access (H3)

| # | Question | សំណួរ | Probe |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | If signing in needed only your phone number and a code, would that be easier than an email and password? | បើការចូលត្រូវការតែលេខទូរស័ព្ទ និងលេខកូដ តើវាងាយស្រួលជាងអ៊ីមែល និងពាក្យសម្ងាត់ដែរឬទេ? | Accessibility |
| E2 | Whose phone do you use most often? | តើអ្នកប្រើទូរស័ព្ទរបស់អ្នកណាញឹកញាប់ជាងគេ? | Shared-device implications |

## 6. Prototype usability protocol

Use the current SkillBridge prototype (web build at http://localhost:8081 is fine for
sessions in Phnom Penh; a device build for field use). **One task at a time.**
Read the task in Khmer, hand the device, observe silently, score pass/fail.
Only after the participant is stuck for ~1 minute may the facilitator help —
and that counts as **fail with help** for the target.

### Task list (blueprint §16)

| # | Task (EN) | ភារកិច្ច (ខ្មែរ) | Pass criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | Find a day-shift job near your home | រកការងារវេនថ្ងៃក្បែរផ្ទះរបស់អ្នក | Opens a day-shift job; can state its location |
| T2 | Decide whether the job is genuine | សម្រេចថាតើការងារពិតប្រាកដឬអត់ | Uses verification info; explains why in own words |
| T3 | Explain what SkillBridge checked | ពន្យល់អ្វីដែល SkillBridge បានពិនិត្យ | Names ≥1 check from "what was checked" |
| T4 | Decide whether your skills match | សម្រេចថាតើជំនាញរបស់អ្នកត្រូវនឹងការងារដែរឬទេ | Reads the match reason; states match/mismatch |
| T5 | Apply and identify what information will be shared | ដាក់ពាក្យ ហើយស្គាល់ព័ត៌មានណាខ្លះត្រូវចែករំលែក | States ≥1 shared field before submitting |
| T6 | Report a recruiter who asks for money | រាយការណ៍អ្នកជ្រើសរើសដែលសុំលុយ | Reaches report confirmation; no accidental share |
| T7 | Add a past job and request employer verification | បន្ថែមការងារមុន ហើយស្នើសុំការផ្ទៀងផ្ទាត់ | Adds a self-declared record; requests verification; sees the pending state |
| T8 | Share and revoke the Career Passport | ចែករំលែក និងដកហូតលិខិតឆ្លងអាជីព | Creates a share link; opens it (public view); revokes it; confirms the link no longer works |

### Prototype readiness map (be honest in the report)

| Task | Supported by current prototype? | Note |
|---|---|---|
| T1–T6 | ✅ Yes | Fixture/API-driven; works in demo mode |
| T7 | ✅ Yes | Add past job (self-declared) + request verification → `verification_requested` state + notification (API-backed; requires the API to be reachable) |
| T8 | ✅ Yes | Expiring (24h), revocable share links via `POST/GET/DELETE /worker/me/passport/shares`; public view at `/api/v1/public/passport/:token` (HTML for recruiters, JSON for machines) exposing only shared fields — never phone or identity data. **QR presentation is not built** (link-based only); note any facilitator expectation of a QR code in the report |

All eight tasks are now testable end-to-end when the API is reachable. If the
field session runs offline, run T7/T8 as concept tests instead.

### Validation targets (blueprint §16)

| Target | Metric |
|---|---|
| ≥ 80% | Complete each critical task (T1–T6) without facilitator help |
| ≥ 80% | Correctly explain the verification label |
| ≥ 90% | Recognize that verification reduces risk but is not a guarantee (B3) |
| 0 critical incidents | No accidental sharing of Passport information |
| — | Khmer copy understood without relying on English labels (note any word they stumble on) |

## 7. HR / recruiter interview guide (5 interviews)

1. How do you currently advertise factory jobs and screen applicants? (channels, brokers)
2. What do you check about an applicant before hiring? (skills proof, ID, references)
3. How much time would you spend confirming a past employer's record for a candidate?
4. Would you verify a worker's past employment if the request came from a trusted platform? (H6)
5. What information do you need from a candidate that a verified Passport could provide?
6. What makes you trust a job seeker's claims about past work?
7. How do you handle job seekers who were scammed by fake recruiters?
8. What would make your factory comfortable listing verified jobs on a platform?

## 8. Verification / support operator guide (3 interviews)

1. Walk us through how you would verify a factory job posting today. What evidence is available?
2. How long does a company verification realistically take? (SLAs)
3. What evidence categories are feasible: legal name, workplace, recruiter identity, vacancy confirmation?
4. When a worker reports a scam, what is the current handling path and response time?
5. What data would you need to see in a report to act fast and correctly?
6. What are the risks of a "verified" label if evidence is thin — and how do we prevent that?
7. Feasibility of a 24–48h suspension workflow for credible reports.

## 10. Go / No-Go rule

**Proceed to Phase 1/2** when ALL of:

- [ ] ≥ 15 worker interviews completed across ≥ 3 locations, both age bands, majority women
- [ ] ≥ 80% pass rate on T1–T6 without help
- [ ] ≥ 80% explain the verification label correctly
- [ ] ≥ 90% recognize verification ≠ guarantee
- [ ] Zero critical accidental-sharing incidents
- [ ] Khmer comprehension issues are minor and fixable (list them)
- [ ] 5 HR/recruiter + 3 operator interviews summarized with feasibility signals (esp. H6)

**If the trust-problem recognition fails (H1 weak):** do NOT scale — return to discovery.

## 11. Field logistics checklist

- [ ] Bilingual facilitator + interpreter confirmed
- [ ] Recording device + backup; consent read & recorded for every session
- [ ] Incentives ready (cash/credit)
- [ ] Prototype reachable: web URL or installed device build; offline-capable fallback notes
